From setup to confident daily use.

At Hillel Academy, Farika facilitated a hands-on new-teacher workshop spanning Google Classroom and Rediker TeacherPlus Gradebook. Participants built and organised classes, tested assignments from a student’s point of view, practised feedback and return, and worked through accurate gradebook setup and reporting.
